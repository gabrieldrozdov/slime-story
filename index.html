the slime things

once upon a time everything was dry. the dryness was unbearable. everyone was always sunburnt.

but then a great sliming occurred.

it was not good at first. many people struggled to adjust to the humidity. dogs had a really bad time because of all the fur.

but then the results became clear. the slime was here to help the humans. the humans were better off in the end. it was a good time and it was even better than before.

every year we celebrate slime day on the first tuesday of october to remember those we lost and all that we gained as a newfound slime society.
